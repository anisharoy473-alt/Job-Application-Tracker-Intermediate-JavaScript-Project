About CareerTrack

WHO WE ARE

A Smarter Way to Manage Your Job Search

CareerTrack is a job application management platform designed to make the job search process easier and more organised. Searching for jobs can become confusing when applications, company details and interview updates are stored in different places.

Our platform brings everything together in one place. Users can explore job opportunities, apply for positions and manage their application status through a personal dashboard.
